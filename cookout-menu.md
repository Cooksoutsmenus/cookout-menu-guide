# Cookout Menu Guide

Cookout is a popular fast-food restaurant known for burgers, barbecue, sides, trays, and a wide variety of milkshake flavors.

## Cookout Menu Categories

### Burgers
Cookout offers several burger options with different toppings and combinations. Customers can choose a burger according to their preferences.

### Sides
Popular Cookout sides include fries, hushpuppies, onion rings, and other classic fast-food options.

### Tray Menu
The Cookout Tray is a popular choice because it combines a main item with sides and a drink or milkshake option.

### Milkshakes
Cookout is well known for its large selection of milkshake flavors. The available flavors may vary by location.

## Menu Prices and Nutrition

Cookout menu prices and nutritional information can vary by location and may change over time. Check current information before ordering.
